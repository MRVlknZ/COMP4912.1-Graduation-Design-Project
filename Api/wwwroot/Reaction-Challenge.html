Aim Reaction Challenge

This challenge is designed like a shooting reflex trainer. Click only when you see the green target. On mobile, tap the green target.

  • Green target: +1 score
  • Bomb target: game over
  • Fake colors appear after a while. Clicking fake color gives -1 score.
  • Targets become faster as time passes.
  • Total challenge duration: 5 minutes
